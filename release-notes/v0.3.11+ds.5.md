## What changes

This release is the price curve: storage costs 100 DARWIN per GB of full chunks on empty nodes, and the price rises to 10 times that at 2,000 records instead of 6,000 (Dan, 2026-10-28 decision: "make chunk price 100 darwin = 1gb", "update to D = 2000"). Every quote check accepts a price on this curve or on the earlier one, so the client works with nodes on either side of the nodes' switch. How payments are made, read back and recovered doesn't change.

### The curve

- **The formula is upstream's,** price = BASELINE + 9 × BASELINE × (n / D)², with n the records a node stores (its weight total, in full chunks). The vendored `calculate_price` now has BASELINE = 0.1396736 DARWIN (139,673,600,000,000,000 wei) and D = 2,000.
- **Where the baseline comes from:** 100 DARWIN per 10^9 bytes × 4,190,208 bytes per full chunk ÷ 3 (a chunk pays 3 × the median quote) = 0.1396736 DARWIN per quote at an empty node. The coefficient stays 9 × the baseline, as upstream, and D = 2,000 makes the price reach 10 × the baseline at 2,000 records.

  | Records stored | Price per quote | Per full chunk | Per GB of full chunks | Earlier curve, per GB |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0 | 0.1396736 DARWIN | 0.4190208 DARWIN | 100 DARWIN | about 89.5 DARWIN (0.125 a quote, 0.375 a chunk) |
  | 2,000 | 1.396736 DARWIN | 4.190208 DARWIN | 1,000 DARWIN | about 179 DARWIN |
  | 6,000 | 11.4532352 DARWIN | 34.3597056 DARWIN | 8,200 DARWIN | about 895 DARWIN |

- **Records by size** pay as in v0.3.11+ds.4: 3 × the median quote × the weight of the record's size class. A 16 KiB-class chunk costs 0.1059828 DARWIN on empty nodes, so the README's 8 KB example, four such chunks, costs 0.4239 DARWIN.
- **The earlier curve** is upstream's constants × 32 (BASELINE 0.125 DARWIN, D 6,000), what nodes to darwin-node 0.23.0+ds.19 quote. It stays in the vendored crate as `calculate_price_legacy`, with `price_band_legacy` and `price_in_band_legacy` beside it; `price_in_band` checks the new curve's band only, and `price_in_accepted_band` accepts a price in either curve's band.

### Accepting both curves

- **Every quote check** (native, merkle and browser) accepts a price in the band of the new curve or in the same band of the earlier curve: from the curve's price at floor(259/1,024 × K) to its price at K, for K committed records. Each band is checked whole, exactly as in v0.3.11+ds.4; a price between the two bands, or past both, is refused, and its error names both bands.
- **So quotes from every node pass:** darwin-node 0.23.0+ds.19 nodes, which quote the earlier curve; darwin-node 0.23.0+ds.20 nodes before their switch, which quote the earlier curve too; and ds.20 nodes after it, which quote the new curve.

### What stays

Settlement version 2, the size classes and their weights, the band, the price ceiling, occupied-quote handling, the merkle refusal, payment recovery, saved-upload restore and envelopes by default are as in v0.3.11+ds.4. `darwin file cost` prices from live quotes, so it shows the earlier prices before the switch and the new ones after it.

### The price ceiling

`--max-chunk-price` (and `ClientConfig::max_chunk_price`) still compares a quote's price, and there is still no default ceiling. A ceiling set near the earlier prices, under about 0.14 DARWIN (139,673,600,000,000,000 base units), drops every new-curve quote after the switch, and uploads then fail for lack of quotes: raise it before the switch.

### Old clients

v0.3.11+ds.4 and older clients check quotes against the earlier curve only, so they refuse every new-curve quote and can't store through ds.20 nodes after the switch. Dan accepted this: those clients are cut off.

### Library users: ant-protocol from the same tag

As before, a project that uses `ant-core` takes both lines below, with the same tag:

```toml
[dependencies]
ant-core = { git = "https://github.com/Project-DSpace/darwin-client", tag = "v0.3.11+ds.5" }

[patch.crates-io]
ant-protocol = { git = "https://github.com/Project-DSpace/darwin-client", tag = "v0.3.11+ds.5" }
```

`ant_protocol::payment::calculate_price` is the new curve. `ant_protocol::payment::pricing` adds `calculate_price_legacy`, `price_band_legacy`, `price_in_band_legacy` and `price_in_accepted_band`; `price_in_band` keeps its meaning, the new curve's band only. The vendored `pricing.rs`, `payment/mod.rs` and `VENDORED.md` are to be the same files as darwin-node 0.23.0+ds.20's: before this release is tagged, a diff of the three files across the two repositories is empty.
